Finding 1 of 3

Match the address everywhere.

What we saw

Your website and Google show 1180 Iron Point Road, Suite 240. Birdeye and LinkCentre show 1130, Suite 138.

Why it matters

Someone could plan the wrong arrival.

Our fix

We’d confirm your details, request directory fixes, and keep your phone, suite, hours and booking link in sync.

Finding 2 of 3

Give service links clear names.

What we saw

Some homepage service links say ‘[LINK]’ rather than naming the service.

Why it matters

People using software that reads links aloud may not know where each one goes.

Our fix

We’d name each service, then check the links by phone, keyboard and screen reader.
